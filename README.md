**Tasbeeh** is your daily companion for performing Adhkar with ease and tranquility, built with **Material Design 3 Expressive**, featuring a clean, modern interface with smooth animations.

## 📱 Key Technical Features
* **UI/UX:** Uses Material Design 3 components, providing a calming and modern experience with highly responsive touch feedback.
* **Core Functionality:**
    * Precise and reliable counter for your daily Adhkar.
    * Lightweight architecture ensuring zero impact on device battery or performance.
* **Interactions (Animations):**
    * Smooth, expressive animations that provide tactile feedback during the counting process.

## 🧩 App Highlights
1. **Calm Interface:** Designed specifically to reduce eye strain and promote focus during remembrance.
2. **High Precision:** Engineered to ensure every click is registered accurately.
3. **Always Improving:** Regular updates with new features and enhancements.

## 🛠 Tools & Libraries
* **Language:** Kotlin.
* **Environment:** Android Studio.
* **Design System:** Material Design 3 Expressive.
